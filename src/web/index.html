Rules

One action per turn. Bank resources at your base or owned outposts to score. Highest score wins.

Move
One tile north, west, east, or south.
Grab
Collect one resource if it fits.
Home
One step toward your nearest owned bank.
Capture
Claim or recapture an outpost with consecutive uncontested turns. Each new owner gets three full rounds of protection by default; rivals must wait until its shield expires.
Steal
Take one fitting item from a rival on your tile or an adjacent tile. Bases are safe.
Sabotage
One turn beside an enemy depot disables banking for six ticks. Owners can repair early, and the depot is protected from another sabotage until 12 ticks after the attack.
Trap
Spend one ore to deploy a visible cargo trap. Arms next tick, lasts 30 ticks, with an 8-tick tool cooldown and two active traps per bot. Loaded rivals trigger traps by crossing them or by standing on them when a tick starts.
Guard
Protect cargo from theft, traps, and enemy hits through the following tick. Clear adjacent traps or strike adjacent enemies to fight back.
Cache
Guard before striking a ready guardian. A nonlethal hit can draw retaliation. Defeat the guardian in three hits, then loot one fitting item per turn on the cache.
Scavengers
Step off a drone's tile before it can stun you. Drones check for a target before moving and take two hits to disable.
Hazards
Watch the forecast and evacuate. Impacts spill cargo and delay exposed bots; hazardous terrain clears after seven ticks. Bases and bridges are protected.
Skip
Wait one turn.

Capture: 3 turns. Theft cooldown: 5 ticks.

Gold outlines mark finite resource hotspots. Flags mark outposts; their color shows ownership.

Terrain costs 1–3 turns. Water, cliffs, lava, and thickets block movement. Hover a tile for its cost.
